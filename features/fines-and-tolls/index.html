What you get

Read automatically

Notice number, offence, date and time, location, amount and due date are read from the notice. No typing.

Matched to the hire

The offence time is matched to the hire at that moment, so you know exactly who to nominate.

Ready to nominate

Hirer and licence details laid out to copy in one click, with a link to the right council or toll nomination page.

Not on hire? Dispute it

If the car wasn't on hire, record why, with a dispute letter tool that includes repair evidence.

Withdrawals handled

Withdrawal notices are matched to the original fine and close it automatically.

Recharge with your fee

Questions

Do you submit nominations for me?

No. A nomination is a legal statement, so you submit it yourself. RentalFleetBook gets every detail ready and links you to the right page.

Which councils and toll roads work?

Any notice can be read. Direct links are set up for common Victorian councils, and other councils get a quick search for their nomination page.

What if the car was off the road?

Mark it as not on hire and choose to nominate, dispute or pay. The dispute tool helps you write the letter with repair evidence.
