What you get

One record per vehicle

Rego and state, make, model, VIN, weekly rate, bond, km limit, service interval and documents, all on the car's page.

Rego and insurance due dates

See which cars have rego or insurance coming up. VicRoads renewal notices are read automatically and the bill goes to Xero.

Fleet utilisation reports

Choose any dates and see days on hire, idle days and rent earned per car and for the whole fleet, with a CSV download.

Documents filed automatically

Forward rego notices, insurance papers, tolls and mechanic invoices to your own inbox address. Each one is read and filed against the right car.

Your team, with roles

Owner, manager, finance and yard staff each see what they need. Add as many users as you like.

Works in the yard

Check a car, start a hire or record a return from your phone. Install it to your home screen like an app.

How it works

Step 1

Import your cars

Step 3

Run your fleet

Hires, servicing and billing all hang off each car.

Questions

Can I import my existing cars?

Yes. Import from Xero or a spreadsheet, or add them one by one. Most fleets are set up in under an hour.

Does it track kilometres?

Yes. Odometer readings come from pickups, returns, mechanic invoices and GPS trackers, so the current reading stays up to date.

Can I see which cars earn the most?

Yes. The fleet utilisation report shows days on hire, idle days and estimated rent per car for any dates you choose.
