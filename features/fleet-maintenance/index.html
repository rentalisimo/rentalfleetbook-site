Questions

Where do odometer readings come from?

Pickup and return inspections, mechanic invoices and GPS trackers. Readings of zero are ignored.

Does it work with any mechanic?

Yes. Any invoice that shows the car's rego and the work done can be read.

Can I record repairs as well as services?

Yes. Services, repairs, tyres and parts are all part of the car's history.
