Questions

Which trackers work?

Most common GPS trackers that can send data to a standard tracking server. Contact us with your tracker model and we'll check.

Is GPS tracking legal for rental cars?

Generally yes, when the hirer is told in the rental agreement. Make sure your agreement includes a tracking clause.

Do I need GPS to use RentalFleetBook?

No. GPS is optional; every other feature works without it.
