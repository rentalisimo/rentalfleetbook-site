What you get

Your own wording

Upload your agreement as Word or PDF. Your wording is kept exactly as it is; names, dates, vehicle and amounts become blanks.

Pickup and return inspections

Photos of every side of the car, odometer and fuel, with damage marked as existing or new. Done on a phone in the yard.

Bond statement on return

When the car comes back, damage, unpaid rent, extra kilometres and fines are pulled into a bond statement you can adjust.

Emailed automatically

The signed agreement and the insurer's papers (policy, PDS, FSG) are emailed to the hirer, with a copy to you.

Licence and hirer details

Licence photos and details stored privately with the customer record, ready for the next hire and for fine nominations.

Billing starts on signing

Signing can create the first invoice and the weekly repeating invoice in Xero, so billing starts the same day.

Questions

Is an on-screen signature valid?

Electronic signatures are generally accepted for commercial agreements in Australia. If you prefer, print the filled-in agreement and sign on paper, then tick it as signed.

Can I keep using my current agreement?

Yes. You upload your own agreement, and its wording stays exactly as you wrote it.

What does the hirer receive?

An email with their signed agreement and the insurance papers for the car they're driving. You get a copy too.
