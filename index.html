Everything a rental business runs on

Made by a Melbourne rental company for its own fleet of 30+ cars, now open to other operators.

📝

Hires & agreements

Your own hire agreement, signed on screen or on paper, then emailed to the hirer with the car's insurance papers. Inspections with photos at pickup and return.

💳

Invoices with Xero

On signing, the first week plus bond is invoiced in Xero and the weekly repeating invoice is set up for you. Every invoice and payment shows on the hire and the customer.

⏰

Overdue reminders

See who owes what at a glance. SMS and email reminders can go out automatically, with the total owing.

🎫

Fines & tolls

Scan or forward a notice: it's read, matched to whoever had the car, and ready to nominate on the council or toll site. Recharge it with your admin fee in one invoice. Disputes and withdrawals too.

🔧

Servicing

Forward the mechanic's invoice and the service, odometer and next due date are filled in, with a draft bill in Xero. Overdue services stand out.

📍

GPS & kilometres

Live map, routes and trips, and kilometres driven per car each week. Works with common GPS trackers.

📥

One inbox for paperwork

Your own forwarding address for tolls, fines, rego notices, insurance and mechanic invoices. Each one is read and filed against the right car or hire.

🚗

Fleet & rego

Every car with its rego, insurance, service plan and documents. VicRoads renewals read automatically, with the bill sent to Xero.

📱

On your phone

Works in any phone browser, and installs to your home screen like an app. Your team gets their own logins and roles.

Questions

Do I need Xero?

It's recommended, as invoices, payments and customers sync with it automatically. You can start without it and connect it later.

Does it work outside Victoria?

Yes. Fines, tolls and hires work anywhere in Australia. Some time-savers, such as reading VicRoads rego notices and Victorian toll and council links, are tuned for Victoria first.

Can my staff use it?

Yes. Add your team with their own logins and roles (owner, manager, finance or staff) at no extra cost.

Where is my data kept?

In Australia (Sydney), encrypted, with daily backups. Licence photos and documents are private to your company.

What happens after the free month?

Add a card in Billing at any time. You'll be charged monthly by fleet size. If you don't add one, your account pauses and nothing is charged.

Try it with your own fleet

Your first month is free, and it takes about ten minutes to set up.
