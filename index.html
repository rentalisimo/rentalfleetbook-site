What the AI does

Paperwork in. Answers out.

Forward or photograph a document and RentalFleetBook's AI reads it in seconds, pulls out what matters and puts it in the right place: the right car, the right hire, the right hirer.

FAQ

Questions operators ask

What does the AI actually do?

It reads documents for you: infringement and toll notices, mechanic invoices, rego notices, insurance papers and driver licence photos. It pulls out the details, files them against the right car and hire, and fills in the forms. You always see what it read and can correct it before anything is relied on.

Do I need Xero?

Xero is recommended, because customers, invoices, repeating invoices and payments then sync automatically. You can start without it and connect it later.

Does it work outside Victoria?

Yes. Hires, agreements, billing, servicing and GPS work anywhere in Australia. A few time-savers, such as reading VicRoads renewal notices and Victorian council and toll links, are tuned for Victoria first.

Can I use my own rental agreement?

Yes. Upload your agreement as a Word file or PDF. Your wording stays exactly as it is; the hirer's details, vehicle, dates and amounts become blanks that fill in for each hire.

Can my staff use it?

Yes. Add your team with their own logins and roles (owner, manager, finance or yard staff) at no extra cost.

Where is my data kept?

In Australia (Sydney region), encrypted, with daily backups. Licence photos and documents are private to your company.

What happens after the free month?

Add a card in Billing at any time and you're charged monthly by fleet size. If you don't, your account pauses and nothing is charged.
