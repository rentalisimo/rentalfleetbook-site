About us

Built by a rental operator, for rental operators

RentalFleetBook comes from a Melbourne car rental business, not a software company.

Why we built it

We run a fleet of more than 30 cars on weekly hires in Melbourne. As the fleet grew, the paperwork grew faster: agreements to print and file, weekly invoices to create in Xero, fines to match to the right hirer and nominate, services and rego dates to track, and overdue rent to chase.

The rental software we looked at was either built for airport car hire with hundreds of vehicles, or was a generic fleet tool that didn't understand hires, bonds and nominations. So we built our own, and we run our fleet on it every day.

What it does today

RentalFleetBook handles the whole life of a hire: the agreement and pickup inspection, invoicing in Xero, fines and tolls during the hire, servicing, GPS, and the return inspection and bond statement at the end. Paperwork such as mechanic invoices, toll statements, infringement notices and rego renewals is forwarded to one inbox, read automatically and filed against the right car.
