What it handles

Six jobs your AI fleet manager does every day

Fines and tolls

Reads the notice number, offence, date and time, place, amount and due date. Then finds who had the car at that moment and gets the nomination ready.

Mechanic invoices

Reads the work done, parts, cost and odometer, updates the car's service history and next service, and drafts the bill in Xero.

Rego notices

Reads VicRoads and other state renewal notices and receipts: due date, amount and which car, with the bill drafted in Xero.

Driver licences

Snap the front and back of a licence. AI fills in name, licence number, expiry, date of birth and address, including change-of-address stickers.

Your rental agreement

Upload your agreement once. AI keeps your wording and turns the hirer, vehicle, dates and amounts into blanks that fill in for every hire.

Your paperwork inbox

Everything you forward is sorted by type (fine, toll, invoice, rego, insurance, withdrawal) and filed against the right car or hire.

How it works

Forward it, check it, done

Step 1

Forward or snap

Email the document to your company's RentalFleetBook inbox address, or take a photo with your phone.

Step 2

AI reads and matches

The AI reads it, works out what it is, and links it to the right car, hire and hirer.

Step 3

You check and act

See what it read next to the original. Confirm, correct if needed, then nominate, pay or recharge.

You stay in control

AI that does the work, not the deciding

Your AI fleet manager is built to save time, not to take decisions out of your hands:

  • Everything it reads is shown with the original document, ready to check and correct
  • The AI never submits a nomination, pays a fine or contacts a hirer because of something it read
  • Billing, reminders and bonds follow the rules you set
  • Documents are read by Anthropic's commercial AI service, which doesn't use them to train its models, and stored privately in Australia

Like any AI, it can occasionally misread a blurry photo or an unusual document. That's why you always get the final say.

Questions about the AI

Is it really AI?

Yes. RentalFleetBook uses Claude, a large language model made by Anthropic, to read documents and photos: infringement and toll notices, mechanic invoices, rego notices, insurance papers, driver licences and your own rental agreement.

How accurate is it?

Very good on clear documents, but no AI is perfect. That's why everything it reads is shown to you, with the original document alongside, so you can check and correct it. Nothing it reads is submitted to a council, a hirer or Xero without you.

Is my data used to train the AI?

No. Documents are sent to Anthropic's commercial API only to be read. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, that data isn't used to train its models. Your documents themselves are stored privately in Australia.

Does the AI cost extra?

No. AI reading is included in every plan, with no per-document fees.

Do I need to set anything up?

No. Forward documents to your company's inbox address, or upload a photo, and the AI does the rest.
