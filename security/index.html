Security

Your data, kept safe in Australia

Rental businesses hold sensitive information: licences, addresses, payments. Here's how RentalFleetBook protects it.

Hosted in Australia

Your database and files are stored in the Sydney region.

Encrypted

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest.

Separated by company

Database rules keep each company's data private to that company. Documents such as licence photos are only accessible to your team.

Role-based access

Owner, manager, finance and yard roles limit what each person can see and change.

Backups

Daily database backups, plus a separate encrypted weekly backup of data and documents.

Payments by Stripe

Card payments for your subscription are handled by Stripe. We never see or store full card numbers.

Xero via secure sign-in

Xero is connected with Xero's own sign-in. We never see your Xero password, and you can disconnect at any time.

Your data stays yours

Ask for an export of your data at any time, or for it to be deleted when you leave.

AI reading, privately

Documents are read by Anthropic's commercial AI service, which doesn't use your data to train its models.

Questions about security or privacy? Read our privacy policy or contact us.
