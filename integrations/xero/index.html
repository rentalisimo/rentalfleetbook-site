What you get

First invoice on signing

When the agreement is signed, the first invoice (week one plus bond) is raised in Xero, dated that day.

Weekly repeating invoices

A weekly repeating invoice is set up from day seven, using your usual accounts and tax settings. It stops when the car is returned.

Overdue reminders

One list of everyone who owes, with SMS and email reminders that show the total owing.

Customers synced

Hirers become Xero contacts with their details. Existing Xero customers and invoices are imported.

Fines and costs recharged

Recharge fines, tolls and damage with your admin fee in a single invoice.

Bills from your inbox

Mechanic invoices and rego renewals arrive as draft bills in Xero, ready to approve.

How it works

Step 1

Import

Step 3

Bill automatically

New hires are invoiced from the day they're signed.

Questions

Do I need Xero to use RentalFleetBook?

No, but it's recommended. Without Xero you still get hires, agreements, fines, servicing and GPS; invoicing is where Xero saves the most time.

Will it change my existing Xero invoices?

No. Existing invoices and repeating invoices are read and shown on the right hire and customer. New ones are created only for new hires.

What happens when a car comes back?

When you return the car in RentalFleetBook, its weekly repeating invoice in Xero is deleted, so the hirer isn't billed again.
