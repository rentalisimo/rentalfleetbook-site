Pricing questions

How is the price worked out?

By the number of vehicles in your account: up to 5 cars $30, 6 to 10 cars $45, 11 to 20 cars $110, and over 20 cars $5 per car. All prices are per month plus GST.

What happens when I add or remove cars?

Your package adjusts automatically on your next monthly invoice. There's nothing to change yourself.

Is there a setup fee or contract?

No setup fee and no lock-in contract. Pay monthly by card and cancel any time.

What's included?

Every feature, unlimited users, Xero integration, email support and your data hosted in Australia.

Do I need a card to start the free month?

No. Start your free month without a card. Add one in Billing when you're ready to keep going.
